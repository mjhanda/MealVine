## The short version

Everything you enter in Dishcart (your plan, recipes, grocery list, notes,
and preferences, including allergies) stays on your device. There is no
account. Dishcart is free; an optional subscription removes ads. The only
network activity is checking that subscription, through Apple or Google and
our subscription provider, RevenueCat. We never sell your data and the app
contains no analytics.

## What the app stores, and where

On your device only, in the app's private storage:

- Your preferences: household size, diet, **allergies**, ingredients you
  avoid, and which meals you plan
- Your meal plan, cooking history, favorites, recipe notes and ratings
- Your grocery list, check-offs, and pantry choices

Your allergy list counts as health-related information. It is used only on
your device to hide recipes, and it never leaves your device unless you
export a backup yourself (see below).

## Ad-free subscription

Dishcart is free to use. An optional **$0.99/month** subscription removes
ads. Payment is processed entirely by the App Store or Google Play; we never
see your payment details. Promotional codes are redeemed through Apple's or
Google's own redemption screens.

To know whether your subscription is active, the app uses **RevenueCat**, a
subscription service. RevenueCat receives a random, anonymous app user ID
created on your device, your purchase and subscription history from the
store, and basic device information (such as platform, OS version, app
version, and country/currency). It does **not** receive your name, email,
meal plan, preferences, or allergies. See RevenueCat's privacy policy:
https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

You can manage or cancel your subscription in your App Store or Google Play
account settings.

## What else leaves your device

Nothing, automatically. Apart from the subscription check above, the app
makes no network requests and contains no analytics, advertising, or
crash-reporting software.

Two actions send data only when **you** choose to, and only where you send it:

- **Share grocery list:** sends the list's text to whichever app you pick
  in your device's share sheet.
- **Export backup:** creates a file containing everything listed above,
  including your allergies, and hands it to the app or location you choose.
  Keep it private.

## Notifications

If you start a cooking timer, the app asks for permission to show a local
notification when the timer ends. Local notifications are scheduled on your
device; no notification service or server is involved.

## Deleting your data

Settings → **Reset all data** erases everything immediately. Uninstalling the
app also removes it. We never receive your meal data, so there is nothing for
us to delete on our side. To delete subscription records held by RevenueCat,
contact us at the address below.

## Children

The app doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children
under 13.

## Allergy information disclaimer

Recipes are filtered using conservative ingredient tags, but we can't
guarantee any product is free of an allergen. Always check labels.
