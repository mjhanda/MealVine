## The short version

MealVine doesn't collect, transmit, sell, or share any of your data. There
is no account, and the app never connects to our servers or anyone else's.
Everything you enter stays on your device.

## What the app stores, and where

On your device only, in the app's private storage:

- Your preferences: household size, diet, **allergies**, ingredients you
  avoid, and which meals you plan
- Your meal plan, cooking history, favorites, recipe notes and ratings
- Your grocery list, check-offs, and pantry choices

Your allergy list counts as health-related information. It is used only on
your device to hide recipes, and it never leaves your device unless you
export a backup yourself (see below).

## What leaves your device

Nothing, automatically. The app makes no network requests and contains no
analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting software.

Two actions send data only when **you** choose to, and only where you send it:

- **Share grocery list:** sends the list's text to whichever app you pick
  in your device's share sheet.
- **Export backup:** creates a file containing everything listed above,
  including your allergies, and hands it to the app or location you choose.
  Keep it private.

## Notifications

If you start a cooking timer, the app asks for permission to show a local
notification when the timer ends. Local notifications are scheduled on your
device; no notification service or server is involved.

## Deleting your data

Settings → **Reset all data** erases everything immediately. Uninstalling the
app also removes it. Because we never receive your data, there is nothing for
us to delete on our side.

## Children

The app doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children
under 13.

## Allergy information disclaimer

Recipes are filtered using conservative ingredient tags, but we can't
guarantee any product is free of an allergen. Always check labels.
